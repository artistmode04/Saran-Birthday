Our First Meetup

July 8, 2026

After you proposed to me, I knew I wanted my answer to be more than just words on a screen. I wanted to see you, stand in front of you, look into your eyes, and let you know exactly what I felt.
So I planned a little surprise. I didn't tell you I was coming. I just showed up, hoping to make that moment as unexpected and special as the feelings I was carrying with me.
And when you saw me, for a few seconds, you had no words at all. Just that look on your face, that little silence between us… I still remember it like it was yesterday, as if time had paused for just the two of us.
I had brought a small, slightly odd bundle with me too, an Alpenliebe strawberry lollipop, a Cadbury Dairy Milk Bubbly, and a few vanilla Kisses. Nothing fancy, just a few little things I picked out for you.
And looking back, I think that's what made that moment so beautiful. It wasn't grand or perfect. It was simply the two of us, finally standing together, with so much left to say and a whole story waiting to begin.

Every Trip, A Memory

We never lived in the same city, so every time we rode out to meet, it became its own journey. Here are the ones we'll never forget.

🏍️ every ride, a memory 🏍️

Ambanad Estate

July 9, 2026

Cool air, warm sun, a soft drizzle, then rain that couldn't decide how hard to fall, and somehow none of it mattered, because you were right there beside me. We rode out on your favorite Royal Enfield, chasing that shifting sky together. Somewhere deep in a quiet rubber tree forest, time seemed to pause, and we shared our first kiss. I slid two bangles onto your wrist that day, green glass and gold steel, a small promise wrapped in a drizzling sky, followed by more hugs than I could ever count.

Mathoor Aqueduct, Nagercoil

July 10, 2026

That day we simply went to see the place, winding through mountains and past quiet lakes, mist settling over everything. Somewhere along the way, on a small bridge with a view we still talk about, with the rain drizzling softly, we lost ourselves for a moment in a kiss, until we realized someone had spotted us from a distance. We panicked, laughed, and ran like we'd robbed a bank. Still one of the funniest, most us moments we have. On our way back, tucked inside a big rubber tea garden, we found ourselves kissing again, except this time it felt different, slower, like we were actually learning each other. Neither of us wanted to leave that place, but the day was ending and the road home was waiting. We took a bus back to Tirunelveli, sitting close, your hand in mine, your head resting on my shoulder the whole way. And then came the hardest part of the day, letting go of your hand at the end, knowing you were headed home and I still had my own long journey back to Chennai. I remember exactly what that moment felt like, and it wasn't easy. But it's alright. We have a whole life ahead of us to hold hands through every up and down, and never really have to let go again.

My Saran,

The very first time I saw you was at my sister's wedding, on the 2nd of November, 2020. I felt something the moment I saw you, even if I couldn't quite explain what it was back then. After that, we'd talk once in a while, a message here and there on random days, spread out over months, with no real plan behind any of it.

Then years later, out of nowhere, I found out you were coming to Chennai. And something shifted in me that I still can't fully put into words. I cleaned my room, my whole house, and, half joking, even the restroom, something I had genuinely never done before in my life. I just wanted everything to feel right for you, neat and cared for, the way I hoped you'd feel about coming all that way. Looking back now, that's really where our love story began.

Everything since has felt like it was building toward something bigger, through every message, every mile, every visit. And standing here today, I know exactly what I want next.

So here's my promise, plain and simple. I'm choosing this, all of it, again and again. Every uphill, every sharp turn, every storm we don't see coming. Not because loving you is easy, but because it's you. Let's keep traveling far, stumbling sometimes, and lifting each other back up every single time. Let's be each other's home, even from far away, until we're old and grey.

And promise me one thing too, please take care of yourself. Eat well, keep yourself fit, look after your health, so we get to live this life long and strong together. There are still so many adventures waiting for us out there, and I want us both around for every single one of them.

We both love tea more than we probably should, and somehow, in all this time, we still haven't had one really good cup of it together. That's alright. We've got a whole life ahead to get it right, one cup, one terrace, one rainy evening at a time.

And one day, when the roads finally get quiet, I want us right there, hands held, tea between us, rain falling just outside, our old bike resting below instead of carrying us through another storm. No more distance left to chase. Just you and me, exactly where we were always meant to be.

Today is your day, my love. So here's a small piece of ours, built only for you, every road, every memory, every reason I fell for you in the first place. Here's to many more rides, many more places, and a lifetime of reasons to run away laughing, together.
